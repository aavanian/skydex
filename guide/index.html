What Skydex is for

Deciding whom to follow, unfollow or mute on Bluesky, with some data: how an account posts, whether it is still active, and what it talks about. It is not a tool for building block lists.

Open it from any profile with the bookmarklet: drag Skydex to your bookmarks bar, then click it while viewing a profile or post in bsky.app or any web client with /profile/ links.

Account profile

Enter a handle, a DID or a profile link. Skydex reads the account's whole public history.

Kinds of activity
Organic: the account's own posts. Quote: posts quoting another post. Reply: replies in a conversation; continuing one's own thread counts as posting, not replying. Repost: sharing someone else's post as is.
Recency and rate
Last activity, last organic post, and posts per week over the last 90 days next to the lifetime average.
Activity mix per month, and every post over time
Both run up to today, so the silence after an account's last post shows as a gap.
What they talk about
Most used words, hashtags, mentions and linked sites over the analysis window, split between own (organic posts and quote commentary) and shared (posts reposted or quoted). Common words are left out. An account's own domains among linked sites often signal self-promotion.
Content tags (experimental)
With an OpenRouter key, a decision model rates the account's recent organic and quote posts: promotional, snark, political, and political snark (both political and snarky). The clearest examples are listed so you can judge the model. Treat the numbers as hints.

Follows scan

Lists everyone an account follows, read from its own follow records, so it includes accounts Bluesky's app hides. Each account's latest 100 posts, replies and reposts give its numbers.

Statuses
Dormant: nothing in 90 days. No own posts lately: only reposts or replies in 90 days. Deactivated, Deleted, Suspended: shown with the last known handle. Blocks you: their follow is hidden from you by their block. Hidden by a block: hidden by a block but no direct block found, likely a block list.
Unfollowing
Log in to Bluesky from the scan to get an Unfollow button on each row, with a confirmation. It also works for accounts that block you. The login lasts until the tab closes or reloads.
Freshness
Each account's activity is reused from your browser's cache for 24 hours by default. Once a list is shown, Refresh list re-reads who the account follows, for example after unfollowing, and Rescan activity fetches every account's activity again.

Limits

  • Dates are those the posting app wrote; imported or back-dated posts can stretch a history.
  • In the follows scan, repost dates are when Bluesky saw them, and a very active account's rate is shown as a minimum (for example 7.7+) when its latest 100 items all fall within 90 days.
  • Only Bluesky's kinds of records are analysed. Accounts using other atproto apps only show as never posting.

Settings for the analysis window, number of posts, model and cache duration are under Settings. See also privacy and data flow.
